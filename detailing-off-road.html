The Detail

Ride hard. We'll handle the rest.

MotorsLuxe was born from the trails — from coming home tired and facing the mud, dust, and grime on our RZRs and trucks. We built this service so you don't have to.

We remove the dirt of the adventure and restore your machine to showroom condition, ready for the next run.

What's Involved

From trail to showroom.

A typical off-road detail can include:

  • Heavy mud & debris removal
  • Full exterior & chassis cleaning
  • Wheel, tire & suspension detailing
  • Interior & cockpit restoration
  • Roll cage & hard-to-reach cleaning
  • Protective finishing for the next ride
