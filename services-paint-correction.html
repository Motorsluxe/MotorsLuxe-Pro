The Service

Depth, clarity, reflection.

Over time, every finish picks up swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation that dull its shine. Paint correction is a careful, multi-stage machine-polishing process that removes these imperfections rather than hiding them.

The result is a dramatically clearer, glossier, more reflective finish — the way your paint was always meant to look.

What's Involved

A meticulous process.

Paint correction is tailored to your paint's condition and can include:

  • Thorough wash & paint decontamination
  • Paint inspection & assessment
  • Multi-stage machine polishing
  • Swirl & light-scratch removal
  • Gloss & clarity restoration
  • Protective finish to seal the results
