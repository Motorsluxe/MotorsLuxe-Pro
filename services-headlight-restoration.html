The Service

Clarity restored.

Sun and age cause headlight lenses to yellow, haze, and oxidize — dimming your output and aging the look of your vehicle. Our restoration process removes that cloudiness and brings back clear, like-new lenses.

The payoff is twofold: better visibility at night, and a noticeably fresher front end.

The Benefits

Brighter & safer.

  • Clears haze & cloudiness
  • Improves night-time visibility
  • Restores like-new clarity
  • Refreshes your vehicle's look
  • Protective finish applied
